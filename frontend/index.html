What is pneumonia?

Pneumonia is an infection that inflames the air sacs in one or both lungs. The air sacs may fill with fluid or pus, leading to cough with phlegm, fever, chills, and difficulty breathing. Severity can range from mild to life-threatening.

This interface helps demonstrate how deep learning models can assist with screening on chest X-rays by estimating the likelihood of pneumonia patterns.
